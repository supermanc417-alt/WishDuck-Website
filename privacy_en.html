WishDuck Privacy Policy

WishDuck is a family growth and wish motivation app. Privacy is part of the product design: we minimize data collection, keep the first version local-first, and do not turn children's behavior into advertising data.

Effective Date: July 27, 2026

1. Summary

WishDuck handles information that a family creates, records, or authorizes in order to provide rules, tasks, stars, wishes, challenges, badges, and purchase entitlements. We do not sell family data, track children for advertising, or design stars as real money.

2. Data We Handle

WishDuck may store family name, child nickname, avatar color, family-member settings, growth rules, recommended tasks, custom tasks, star rewards, completion records, approval records, recognition moments, star history, wishes, challenge runs, challenge dates, parent PIN, local settings, and StoreKit purchase status or local entitlement cache.

3. Local-First Storage

The first version of WishDuck stores data primarily on the user's device. We currently do not provide our own account system and do not upload family content to our own servers. If you send support email, your email client and email provider process the message content.

4. Future iCloud Sync

If a future version provides iCloud sync, we plan to use the user's own Apple/iCloud capabilities where possible. Before sync is enabled, the app will explain the related behavior. iCloud data handling is also governed by Apple's iCloud terms and privacy policy.

5. Children's Privacy

WishDuck is designed for family use and family content is created and managed by parents. We do not require children to create public accounts, provide public child profiles, use children's behavior for ad targeting, or require children to join a public social network.

6. Third-Party Services

The current product direction does not rely on third-party advertising SDKs. Purchases, refunds, purchase restoration, and product price display are handled by Apple App Store / StoreKit. Apple may process purchase and system-service data under its own policies.

We do not sell, rent, or trade family personal data. Third-party processing is limited to what is needed to provide a system capability, honor authorization, comply with law, or protect legitimate rights.

7. What We Do Not Do

  • We do not sell family data.
  • We do not track children for advertising.
  • We do not design stars as real money, a wallet, or a bank balance.
  • We do not integrate third-party advertising analytics SDKs in the first version.
  • We do not upload family content to our own servers.

8. Deletion, Security, And Control

You can delete family data through the in-app local reset feature. Deleting the app or erasing device data may also delete locally stored content. If iCloud sync is introduced later, deletion of synced data will be explained in the app or support documentation based on that sync design.

Please protect your device, Apple ID, system passcode, and parent PIN. The parent PIN is intended to reduce accidental access and should not be understood as a separate high-security encrypted vault.

9. Changes

If product features, sync behavior, purchase behavior, or data handling changes materially, we will update this policy and its effective date.

10. Contact

For deletion requests, privacy questions, or sync-related questions, contact support@wishduck.app.
