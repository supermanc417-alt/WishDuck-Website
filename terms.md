- 判断奖励星星、认可方式和心愿目标是否合适；

Tasks, presets, challenge packs, and special activities are family-practice suggestions. Adjust them based on real circumstances. Do not ask a child to complete tasks that are unsafe, age-inappropriate, unhealthy, or unsuitable for your family.

挑战包开始后会根据选择的开始时间和周期生成每天任务。当天任务全部完成后，当天状态才算完成；全部天数完成后，挑战成功并可解锁对应徽章或奖励。具体玩法可能随版本更新。

When a challenge starts, WishDuck schedules daily tasks based on the selected start date and duration. A day is complete only when all required tasks for that day are done. When all days are complete, the challenge succeeds and may unlock a badge or reward. Gameplay details may change across versions.
