WishDuck Gameplay Details

This page explains how WishDuck connects family recognition, stars, wishes, task challenges, and badges into one simple loop.

1. What WishDuck Is Not

WishDuck is not a financial product, wallet, bank account, accounting tool, or child social network. Stars are family recognition symbols, not money.

2. Core Loop

  1. Parents create a family and add a child.
  2. Parents choose a recommended task or create a custom rule.
  3. A child completes a chore, habit, learning activity, exploration, or recognized moment.
  4. Parents approve the progress or record completion directly.
  5. Approved progress earns stars and moves a wish forward.
  6. Completed wishes, challenges, or milestones unlock badges and family memories.

3. Parent Side And Child Side

Parent Side

Parents manage family members, rules, wishes, star rewards, approvals, direct recording, challenges, PIN, purchases, and the full family view.

Child Side

Children focus on today's tasks, earned stars, wish progress, badges, and optional progress submission.

4. Task Types

Tasks include chores, habits, learning, exploration, family cooperation, kindness, parent recognition, and parent-child moments.

  • Chores: make the bed, help with dishes, fold clothes, sweep, and tidy a desk.
  • Habits: brush teeth, prepare tomorrow's clothes, keep a bedtime rhythm, and reflect.
  • Learning and exploration: read, observe a plant, make a discovery, and practice focus.
  • Family moments: help someone, say thank you, comfort a family member, and share a good moment.

5. Stars And Wishes

Stars come from parent-confirmed growth moments. They help children see the relationship between effort and goals, but they are not real money. Families can start with a few simple tasks and one easy-to-understand wish.

6. Task Challenges

Task challenges are themed daily task plans. When a challenge starts, parents choose the child, duration, start date, and automatically calculated end date.

Daily State

Each day shows its scheduled tasks. A task can be completed from challenge details. A day is complete only when all required tasks for that day are complete.

Challenge Completion

A challenge succeeds when every scheduled day is complete. The completion bonus is awarded once and the related challenge badge is unlocked once.

7. Badges And Achievements

Badges record long-term growth. Achievement directions include star milestones, the first completed wish, task milestones, theme challenge badges, and future streak, cooperation, learning, exploration, and seasonal badges.

8. Free, Pro, And Paid Theme Packs

  • Free: experience the complete core loop with starter rules, wishes, basic tasks, and basic challenges.
  • Pro: unlock broader app capabilities, external Pro tasks, Pro challenge experiences, and future Pro features.
  • Paid theme packs: unlock only the internal challenge content, premium tasks, badge, theme rewards, and related share assets for that pack.
  • Coffee support: optional support that does not unlock core functionality.
