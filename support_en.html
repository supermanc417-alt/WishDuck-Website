Frequently Asked Questions

Does a child have to submit progress?

No. Parents can record completion directly, or let a child submit progress for parent review.

Does the first version need a backend for activities?

No. Activities are bundled in the app and displayed from local dates. Limited-time activities roll annually instead of using a fixed year.

Are theme challenge packs included with Pro?

No. Pro and paid theme challenge packs are separate entitlement lines. Buying a theme pack unlocks only that pack's internal content.
