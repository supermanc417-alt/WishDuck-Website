# 心愿鸭隐私政策 / WishDuck Privacy Policy

生效日期 / Effective Date: 2026-07-27

心愿鸭 WishDuck 是给家庭使用的成长激励与心愿 App。我们把隐私设计成产品的一部分：尽量减少数据收集，第一版优先保存在设备本地，不把儿童行为变成广告数据。

WishDuck is a family growth and wish motivation app. Privacy is part of the product design: we minimize data collection, keep the first version local-first, and do not turn children's behavior into advertising data.

## 1. 我们处理哪些数据 / Data We Handle

心愿鸭可能在你的设备上保存以下家庭内容：

- 家庭名称、孩子昵称、头像颜色和家庭成员设置；
- 成长规则、推荐任务、自定义任务、星星奖励配置；
- 完成记录、审核记录、认可记录和星星流水；
- 心愿名称、所需星星、进度和完成状态；
- 任务挑战、挑战开始时间、每日任务状态、完成奖励和徽章；
- 家长 PIN 码和本地设置；
- StoreKit 返回的购买状态或本地权益缓存。

WishDuck may store the following family content on your device:

- family name, child nickname, avatar color, and family-member settings;
- growth rules, recommended tasks, custom tasks, and star reward settings;
- completion records, approval records, recognition moments, and star history;
- wish names, required stars, progress, and completion state;
- challenge runs, start dates, daily task states, completion rewards, and badges;
- parent PIN and local settings;
- StoreKit purchase status or local entitlement cache.

## 2. 本地优先 / Local-First Storage

第一版心愿鸭主要把数据保存在用户设备本地。我们当前不提供自建账号系统，也不把家庭内容上传到自建服务器。

The first version of WishDuck stores data primarily on the user's device. We currently do not provide our own account system and do not upload family content to our own servers.

## 3. iCloud 同步计划 / Future iCloud Sync

如果后续版本提供 iCloud 同步，我们计划优先通过用户自己的 Apple/iCloud 能力同步家庭数据。启用同步前，App 内会说明相关行为。iCloud 数据处理还会受 Apple 的 iCloud 条款和隐私政策约束。

If a future version provides iCloud sync, we plan to use the user's own Apple/iCloud capabilities where possible. Before sync is enabled, the app will explain the related behavior. iCloud data handling is also governed by Apple's iCloud terms and privacy policy.

## 4. 儿童隐私 / Children's Privacy

心愿鸭面向家庭使用，由家长创建和管理家庭内容。我们不要求儿童创建公开账号，不提供儿童公开主页，不把儿童行为用于广告定向。

WishDuck is designed for family use and family content is created and managed by parents. We do not require children to create public accounts, do not provide public child profiles, and do not use children's behavior for ad targeting.

## 5. 第三方服务 / Third-Party Services

当前产品方向不依赖第三方广告 SDK。购买、退款、恢复购买和商品价格展示由 Apple App Store / StoreKit 处理。发送支持邮件时，你的邮件客户端和邮件服务商会处理邮件内容。

The current product direction does not rely on third-party advertising SDKs. Purchases, refunds, purchase restoration, and product price display are handled by Apple App Store / StoreKit. If you send support email, your email client and email provider process the message content.

## 6. 我们不会做的事 / What We Do Not Do

- 不出售家庭数据；
- 不为广告目的追踪儿童；
- 不把星星设计成真实货币、钱包或银行余额；
- 不要求孩子加入公开社交网络；
- 不在第一版接入第三方广告分析 SDK。

We do not:

- sell family data;
- track children for advertising;
- design stars as real money, a wallet, or a bank balance;
- require children to join a public social network;
- integrate third-party advertising analytics SDKs in the first version.

## 7. 数据删除 / Data Deletion

你可以在 App 内通过本地重置功能删除家庭数据。删除 App 或清除设备数据也可能删除本地保存的内容。若未来启用 iCloud，同步数据的删除方式会根据当时的同步设计在 App 或支持文档中说明。

You can delete family data through the in-app local reset feature. Deleting the app or erasing device data may also delete locally stored content. If iCloud sync is introduced later, deletion of synced data will be explained in the app or support documentation based on that sync design.

## 8. 安全 / Security

我们会尽力使用平台提供的安全能力保护数据。请妥善保护设备、Apple ID、系统锁屏密码和家长 PIN。家长 PIN 用于降低误触风险，不应被理解为独立的高强度加密保险箱。

We use platform-provided security capabilities where appropriate. Please protect your device, Apple ID, system passcode, and parent PIN. The parent PIN is intended to reduce accidental access and should not be understood as a separate high-security encrypted vault.

## 9. 政策更新 / Changes

如果产品功能、同步方式、内购方式或数据处理方式发生重要变化，我们会更新本政策的生效日期和内容。

If product features, sync behavior, purchase behavior, or data handling changes materially, we will update this policy and its effective date.
